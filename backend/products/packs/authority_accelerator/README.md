# Authority Accelerator Pack

For operators who have already built a real practice (2+ years of
clients, a meaningful following, a portfolio of wins) and want to
spend the next 12 months becoming the obvious choice in their niche —
the person podcast hosts call first, the name that gets dropped in
high-stakes RFPs, the writer whose essays get forwarded.

## What this pack does that the others don't

The Creator QuickStart Pack is for finding your voice. The Full
Content Funnel Kit is for turning that voice into pipeline. This
pack is for **positioning your voice as authoritative** — so the
same content drives 5-10x the inbound at the same volume because
of who the audience perceives you to be.

The difference is structural, not aesthetic. Authority is built on:

1. **Repeatable frameworks** that you (and only you) are associated
   with.
2. **Earned channels** — podcasts, press, speaking — where third
   parties vouch for you in front of their audience.
3. **Compounding owned media** — long-form essays, books, deep case
   studies — that an audience can binge-read and emerge as a
   committed fan.
4. **A 12-month editorial arc** that builds toward a credible
   declaration ("I'm the person who solved X for Y kind of
   business").

## How to deploy

### Month 1 — Foundation

1. **Day 1-3:** Work through `02_signature_framework_worksheet.md`.
   This is the keystone — every other asset in the pack derives from
   it. Don't skip ahead.
2. **Day 4-7:** Open `01_twelve_month_editorial_plan.md`. Map your
   four quarterly themes against your existing content backlog.
3. **Day 8-14:** Build the speaker bureau one-pager
   (`04_speaker_bureau_onepager.md`). One day of writing + one day
   of design. Use a designer for the layout — it matters here.
4. **Day 15-30:** Start executing the Q1 editorial plan. Publish on
   schedule.

### Month 2 — Earned channels

1. Pull a list of 50 podcasts in your space. Use
   `03_podcast_pitch_template.md` to send 10 cold pitches per week.
2. Set up a tracking sheet (date sent, status, follow-up date).
3. Expect a 5-15% acceptance rate. Plan for 5-8 bookings from a
   200-pitch quarter.

### Month 3 onward — PR + amplification

1. Open `05_pr_outreach_sequence.md`. Send the cycle 1 outreach to
   3 publications (start small).
2. Continue podcast pitching at 10/week.
3. Execute Q2 editorial plan.

### Months 6-12 — Compound

1. Re-evaluate the signature framework — has it landed? Refine.
2. Pursue paid speaking gigs (the one-pager pays off here).
3. Consider a book proposal if you have ~50,000 words of long-form
   already published.

## What you'll need from outside the pack

- ~2 hours/day of focused writing time, 4 days a week, minimum.
- A designer for the speaker one-pager + any "official" assets ($300-
  $1500 one-time).
- Patience. Authority compounds; it does not spike.

## What's NOT in here

- Specific publication lists (industry-dependent — research your own).
- Paid speaker-rep introductions (relational, not transactional).
- Book proposal templates (separate engagement if you go that route).
- Ghost-writing services (this assumes you're writing your own).

## What success looks like at 12 months

- Your signature framework name is recognizable in your niche.
- You've been a guest on 10-20 relevant podcasts.
- You've placed 2-5 bylined articles in industry publications.
- Your inbound has shifted from "could you do X for me?" to "could
  you speak at our event?" / "I'd like to interview you for…"
- Pricing power: you can charge 30-100% more for the same work
  because the perception of who's doing it has shifted.
