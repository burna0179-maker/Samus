# 14 Ready-to-Post Hook Templates

Use one per day for the first 2 weeks. Each is a structural pattern,
not a script — fill in the **[bracketed]** parts with your actual
content. Each comes with a worked example.

---

## 1. The contrarian take

**Template:**
> Everyone says [common wisdom]. After [specific experience], I think
> they're [wrong / partly right / right but for the wrong reasons].
> Here's what actually happened.

**Example:**
> Everyone says "niche down." After 18 months of niching down, I think
> they're right but for the wrong reasons. Here's what actually
> happened.

---

## 2. The specific number

**Template:**
> [N] [unit] of [activity] taught me [X] things about [topic]. Here's
> the most counterintuitive one.

**Example:**
> 437 cold calls in 60 days taught me 4 things about objections. Here's
> the most counterintuitive one.

---

## 3. The "I used to think"

**Template:**
> I used to think [old belief]. Then [specific event] happened, and now
> I think [new belief].

**Example:**
> I used to think you needed a polished website to land your first
> client. Then I landed 3 clients in a row with a single Notion page,
> and now I think the website is a vanity project for year two.

---

## 4. The single sentence question

**Template:**
> [Specific question that the reader will immediately want to answer
> in their head.]

**Example:**
> If your business stopped getting any new leads tomorrow, how long
> until you'd run out of cash?

---

## 5. The list teaser

**Template:**
> [N] things I wish someone had told me about [topic]:

(Then deliver them. No throat-clearing.)

**Example:**
> 5 things I wish someone had told me about pricing services:

---

## 6. The "this is what I'm doing differently"

**Template:**
> I'm changing how I [do X] starting today. Here's what and why.

**Example:**
> I'm changing how I onboard clients starting today. Here's what
> and why.

---

## 7. The behind-the-scenes

**Template:**
> What [activity] actually looks like — not the version on LinkedIn:

**Example:**
> What "5-figure months" actually looks like — not the version on
> LinkedIn:

---

## 8. The lesson from failure

**Template:**
> The [N]-month lesson I just paid [cost] to learn:

**Example:**
> The 6-month lesson I just paid $40k in lost revenue to learn:

---

## 9. The micro-framework

**Template:**
> My [N]-step framework for [outcome]:

(Then the framework, each step in one sentence.)

**Example:**
> My 3-step framework for closing a discovery call:

---

## 10. The vulnerable admission

**Template:**
> Something I haven't been honest about: [admission].

(Use sparingly — once a month max. Loses power if overused.)

**Example:**
> Something I haven't been honest about: I undercharged my last 3
> clients because I was scared of losing them.

---

## 11. The pattern-interrupt question

**Template:**
> What does [thing nobody talks about] cost you per month?

**Example:**
> What does context-switching cost you per month? I tracked mine for
> 30 days.

---

## 12. The before-and-after

**Template:**
> [N] [time units] ago: [worse state]. Today: [better state]. Here's
> what changed.

**Example:**
> 12 months ago: working 70-hour weeks for $4k/month. Today: 35-hour
> weeks for $14k/month. Here's what changed.

---

## 13. The "if I were starting today"

**Template:**
> If I were starting [thing] from zero today, here's exactly what I'd
> do in week 1:

**Example:**
> If I were starting a consulting practice from zero today, here's
> exactly what I'd do in week 1:

---

## 14. The naming-the-thing post

**Template:**
> The [pattern you've noticed but doesn't have a name yet] is real.
> Here's how to spot it in your own [domain]:

**Example:**
> The "first-quarter false confidence" trap is real. Here's how to
> spot it in your own business:

---

## How to rotate them

Don't use #1 on Monday and #2 on Tuesday — too predictable. Mix:

| Day | Hook # |
|---|---|
| Mon | 9 (framework) |
| Tue | 5 (list) |
| Wed | 7 (behind-the-scenes) |
| Thu | 1 (contrarian) |
| Fri | 12 (before/after) |
| Mon | 3 (used to think) |
| Tue | 2 (specific number) |
| Wed | 13 (if I were starting today) |
| Thu | 11 (pattern-interrupt) |
| Fri | 6 (what I'm doing differently) |
| Mon | 8 (lesson from failure) |
| Tue | 4 (single question) |
| Wed | 14 (naming the thing) |
| Thu | 10 (vulnerable — only if true) |

By the end of 14 posts you'll know which 3 hooks land best for *your*
audience. Lean into those for month 2.

## What kills hooks

- **Hedging.** "I think maybe this might be …" Cut.
- **Throat-clearing.** "So I was thinking about this the other day…"
  Cut.
- **Multiple ideas in the hook.** One idea per post — the hook should
  promise one specific payoff.
- **No specificity.** "Some things I learned" loses to "4 things I
  learned in 437 cold calls."
