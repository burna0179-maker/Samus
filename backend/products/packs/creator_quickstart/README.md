# Creator QuickStart Pack

Day-one operator kit for a solo creator who's tired of staring at a
blank Notion and wants to be shipping content + capturing leads by
end-of-week.

## What's in here

| File | What it is | When you use it |
|---|---|---|
| `01_content_calendar_template.md` | A 4-week editorial calendar in plain markdown — rotate the topics, slot them into your tool of choice | Day 1, before any posting |
| `02_notion_crm_skeleton.md` | The 6-database Notion CRM layout (Contacts, Companies, Deals, Tasks, Notes, Touch Log) | Day 2, before any outreach |
| `03_weekly_review_checklist.md` | The 45-minute Friday review that keeps you from drifting | Every Friday, forever |
| `04_brand_voice_prompt.md` | LLM prompt that locks in your voice so your AI tools don't sound generic | Every time you use an LLM for content |
| `05_first_14_hooks.md` | 14 ready-to-post hook templates, each with a worked example | This week, to break the "what do I post?" paralysis |

## How to deploy

1. **Open the content calendar first.** Pick which 4 topic-pillars you're
   committing to for the next 30 days. Don't pick more than 4 — pillar
   spread is the #1 reason creator calendars fall apart in week 2.
2. **Set up the Notion CRM.** Open Notion, create the 6 databases per
   `02_notion_crm_skeleton.md`, link them with the relations shown.
   30 minutes if you've used Notion before; 90 minutes if you haven't.
3. **Save the brand voice prompt** somewhere you'll actually find it —
   pin it in your Claude / ChatGPT, save it as a snippet, whatever
   you use. Every time you ask AI to draft content, paste this
   prompt first.
4. **Use the 14 hooks** to seed your first 2 weeks of posts. Don't
   over-customize — the templates work because they're proven
   structures, not because of the specific words.
5. **Block 45 minutes every Friday** for the weekly review. Put it on
   your calendar as a recurring event. Treat it the same way you'd
   treat a client meeting — don't skip it.

## What's NOT in here (intentional)

- A specific posting tool (Buffer, Hypefury, Typefully, native scheduler).
  Pick whatever you'll actually open daily.
- Ad templates. This pack is for organic content; paid is a different
  game.
- A specific niche. Every template is voice-agnostic — fill in your
  topic.
