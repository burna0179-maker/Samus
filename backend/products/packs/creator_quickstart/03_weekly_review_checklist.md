# Friday 45-Minute Weekly Review

The single highest-leverage 45 minutes in your week. Block it on your
calendar as a recurring event. Don't skip it.

## Part 1 — Look back (15 min)

### Content audit (5 min)

- [ ] Did I post on the days my calendar said to? Y / N — if N, what
  blocked me?
- [ ] Which post got the most engagement? Why? Write 1 sentence.
- [ ] Which post landed worst? Why? Write 1 sentence.
- [ ] Did I respond to comments + DMs within 24 hours?

### Relationships audit (5 min)

- [ ] Did I touch every Hot contact this week? (Check Notion CRM
  "Stale contacts" view)
- [ ] Any Hot contacts that have gone silent for >10 days? Move to
  Warm with a note explaining why.
- [ ] Any new Hot contacts to add? (From comments, DMs, replies)

### Money + admin audit (5 min)

- [ ] Did I send every invoice that was due this week?
- [ ] Did I collect every invoice that was due this week?
- [ ] Any expenses to log?
- [ ] Any contracts / proposals waiting on a signature?

## Part 2 — Look ahead (20 min)

### Sunday-planning preview (10 min)

Don't wait until Sunday — use the energy of the Friday review to
pre-think next week.

- [ ] What are the 4 pillar topics I'll cover next week? (Just topics,
  not posts yet.)
- [ ] What's the one piece of long-form content I'll ship? (Essay,
  thread, video — pick one and commit.)
- [ ] What's the one new outbound contact I'll add to the CRM?

### Active deals (10 min)

Open Notion CRM → Hot deals view. For each deal:

- [ ] What's the next touch? When?
- [ ] Is anything blocking the deal? If yes, what's *my* next action?
- [ ] Has the prospect gone silent for >5 days? Schedule the next
  touch with a specific date.

Add the next-touch as a Task in the CRM with a date.

## Part 3 — Reflect (10 min)

Open a fresh note in your weekly review template (suggestion below).
Answer these three questions in writing:

1. **What was the highlight of the week?**
   One thing — biggest win, best feeling, proudest moment. Specific.

2. **What was the lowlight?**
   One thing — biggest miss, most-avoided task, most stressful moment.
   Be honest with yourself.

3. **What's the one thing I want to do differently next week?**
   Specific, actionable, measurable. Not "be more productive" — "block
   9-11am for deep work Tue/Wed."

## Why this works

Three reasons this ritual outperforms ad-hoc reviews:

1. **Forcing function.** The same questions get asked weekly — you can't
   selectively avoid the uncomfortable ones.
2. **Pattern recognition.** Six weeks in, you'll see your own patterns
   (e.g. "I always skip outbound on weeks I have a lot of client
   work" — actionable insight you'd never spot otherwise).
3. **Compounding clarity.** Each week's "one change" is small. Over 12
   weeks that's 12 small improvements compounding.

## What kills the ritual

- Trying to do it at the end of the day on Friday when you're tired.
  → Move to Friday morning, fresh brain.
- Making it longer than 45 minutes. Bloat = abandonment.
  → Use a timer. When 45 min is up, stop.
- Skipping a week "because nothing happened."
  → Especially do it on quiet weeks. That's when patterns are easiest
  to spot.
