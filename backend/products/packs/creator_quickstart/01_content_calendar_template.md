# 4-Week Content Calendar Template

## How to use this

Pick **4 topic-pillars** for the next 30 days. These are the recurring
themes you want to be known for. Examples:

- An operator's pillars might be: (1) workflow design,
  (2) tools we evaluate, (3) lessons from past clients,
  (4) industry takes.
- A coach's pillars might be: (1) framework Mondays,
  (2) client transformations, (3) mindset essays,
  (4) tactical how-tos.

Rotate through the pillars so each week covers all 4. This protects
you from the "I posted 5 things about workflow and 0 about clients"
drift.

## The rotation

| Day | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Pillar 1 — long-form essay or thread | Pillar 1 — short hook | Pillar 1 — case study | Pillar 1 — framework reveal |
| Tue | Pillar 2 — tactical post | Pillar 2 — listicle | Pillar 2 — opinion | Pillar 2 — "what I learned this week" |
| Wed | Pillar 3 — story | Pillar 3 — Q&A from comments | Pillar 3 — transformation | Pillar 3 — lessons learned |
| Thu | Pillar 4 — hot take | Pillar 4 — counter-take to industry consensus | Pillar 4 — predictions | Pillar 4 — recap of the week's industry news |
| Fri | Recap thread (best of the week) | Recap thread | Recap thread | Recap thread |
| Sat | Off | Off | Off | Off |
| Sun | Plan next week | Plan next week | Plan next week | Plan next week |

## Per-post checklist

Before you publish anything, run it through this 5-point checklist:

1. **Hook.** Does the first line force a stop-scroll? Use one of the
   14 templates from `05_first_14_hooks.md`.
2. **Specificity.** Is there a number, a name, or a date in the post?
   If everything is abstract, rewrite.
3. **One idea.** Does the post advance one idea, or is it three ideas
   mashed together? Split if needed.
4. **Reader takeaway.** Can the reader summarize what they got in one
   sentence? If not, the post is too long or too vague.
5. **Call to action.** Is there one clear next action — even if that
   action is "reply with your take"? Posts without CTAs leak energy.

## The Sunday planning ritual (15 min)

Every Sunday, look at the upcoming week's calendar row and:

1. For each pillar slot, write down the *topic* (not the post, just
   the topic). 5 minutes total.
2. For each topic, write down a one-line *angle* — the specific take
   you'll have. 5 minutes total.
3. For Mondays + Wednesdays, pre-draft the hook line. Just the hook —
   nothing else. 5 minutes.

Now you're walking into the week with 5 hooks and 5 angles already
locked. The actual writing per post takes 10-15 minutes when you've
done this prep.

## When to deviate

Three valid reasons to skip a slot or move it:

1. **Real-world event in your industry.** A breaking story is worth
   moving a Thursday-pillar-4-hot-take to Monday. React fast.
2. **A genuine client / customer story drops in your lap.** A
   transformation story trumps a planned framework post.
3. **You're burnt out.** Skip a day. The calendar is a tool, not a
   commitment-to-perfection device. Skipping one slot in week 3 to
   protect your mental state for weeks 4+ is the right tradeoff.

Don't skip more than one slot per week. Two skips becomes three becomes
"I haven't posted in 3 weeks."
