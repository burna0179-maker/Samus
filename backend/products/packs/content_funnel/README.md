# Full Content Funnel Kit

A TOFU/MOFU/BOFU asset bundle for operators who are past the "what
do I post?" stage and need to start moving readers from awareness
through to revenue.

## The funnel — quick refresher

| Layer | Audience state | Asset type in this pack |
|---|---|---|
| **TOFU** (top of funnel) | Doesn't know they have your problem | Short-form hooks + long-form outlines |
| **MOFU** (middle of funnel) | Knows the problem, evaluating options | Lead magnet skeleton + nurture sequence |
| **BOFU** (bottom of funnel) | Ready to buy, choosing between options | Decision-stage email sequence + objection-handler bank |

Most creator-stage funnels are 90% TOFU and 0% MOFU/BOFU. That's why
they generate followers and no revenue. This kit balances the funnel.

## What's in here

| File | Layer | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `01_short_form_hooks.md` | TOFU | Stop-scroll posts that earn the first impression |
| `02_long_form_outline_framework.md` | TOFU | Essays / threads / videos that establish authority |
| `03_lead_magnet_skeleton.md` | TOFU → MOFU | The asset that gets you their email |

## How to deploy

### Week 1 — TOFU posting

1. Open `01_short_form_hooks.md`. Pick 5 hook patterns. Post one per
   weekday.
2. Open `02_long_form_outline_framework.md`. Pick 1 outline. Draft 1
   long-form piece. Publish by Friday.

### Week 2 — MOFU asset build

1. Open `03_lead_magnet_skeleton.md`. Build your lead magnet. Should
   take ~6 hours total. It doesn't need to be perfect — v1 is fine.
2. Add the lead-magnet CTA to your bio, your highest-performing
   posts, and the bottom of your long-form pieces.

### Week 3 — MOFU sequence wire-up

1. Open `04_nurture_email_sequence.md`. Customize the 7 emails to
   your offer.
2. Wire the sequence to fire on lead-magnet signup (using
   ConvertKit, MailerLite, Beehiiv, whatever you use — instructions
   in the file).

### Week 4 — Measurement + iteration

1. Open `05_funnel_health_scorecard.md`. Fill in your week-1 numbers
   for each tile.
2. Identify the leak — there is always exactly one biggest leak. Spend
   week 5 fixing it.
