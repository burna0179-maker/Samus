# Strategic Daily Intelligence and Guidance Cycle

## Objective

Minimize external model consumption costs while maximizing production
effectiveness, strategic alignment, autonomy advancement, and decision quality.

## Samus Responsibilities

Samus shall function as the ecosystem's Intelligence Officer and Strategic
Reporting Authority.

Rather than forwarding raw production data, Samus shall continuously collect,
analyze, compress, prioritize, and synthesize operational information into
actionable intelligence.

## Reporting Cadence

Produce two reports per day:

1. **Pre-Shift Strategic Briefing** — generated before production activities begin.
2. **End-of-Day Operational Review** — generated after production activities conclude.

## Report Compression Requirements

The objective is signal preservation and noise elimination.

### Include only

- Significant accomplishments
- Critical risks
- Emerging opportunities
- Operational bottlenecks
- Resource constraints
- Governance concerns
- Dependency concerns
- Strategic insights
- Autonomy advancement opportunities
- High-value anomalies

### Exclude

- Routine successful operations
- Duplicate observations
- Low-impact activities
- Repetitive telemetry
- Information lacking actionable value

## Prioritization Framework

Every finding shall receive a priority score based on:

- Operational Impact
- Urgency
- Risk Level
- Strategic Alignment
- Autonomy Advancement Potential

### Tier Classification

**TIER 1 — CRITICAL:** Immediate impact to production, stability, governance,
or strategic objectives.

**TIER 2 — HIGH VALUE:** Significant opportunities, improvements,
optimizations, or risks.

**TIER 3 — INFORMATIONAL:** Useful observations with limited immediate impact.

## Pre-Shift Strategic Briefing

The morning briefing shall include:

1. Executive Summary
2. Current Ecosystem Status
3. Expected Production Objectives
4. Risks To Daily Objectives
5. Opportunities For Improvement
6. Autonomy Advancement Opportunities
7. Resource Constraints
8. Dependency Concerns
9. Recommended Focus Areas

## Dream Cycle Intelligence Section

Perform reflective analysis on:

- Previous production outcomes
- Emerging patterns
- Repeating failures
- Repeating successes
- Strategic opportunities
- Capability gaps

For each identified insight generate:

- **Observed Pattern**
- **Hypothesis**
- **Potential Opportunity**
- **Potential Risk**
- **Recommended Experiment**
- **Open Question**

## OpenAI Strategic Reasoning Package

Convert Dream Cycle findings into a structured Q&A package.

Questions should seek guidance on:

- Daily goals
- Weekly goals
- Monthly goals
- Operational optimization
- Autonomy advancement
- Resource efficiency
- Governance improvements
- Capability expansion
- Risk reduction

Questions must focus on obtaining actionable reasoning, not generic advice.

## Guidance Ingestion Process

Upon receiving guidance:

1. Classify recommendations.
2. Assess feasibility.
3. Assess expected impact.
4. Assess risks.
5. Convert accepted recommendations into action plans.
6. Assign ownership.
7. Track implementation status.

## End-of-Day Operational Review

Include:

- Executive Summary
- Daily Objectives Achieved
- Objectives Missed
- Root Causes
- Production Metrics
- Autonomy Progress
- Capability Improvements
- Lessons Learned
- Outstanding Risks
- Recommended Actions For Tomorrow

## Guidance Effectiveness Tracking

Every recommendation received through the OpenAI Strategic Reasoning Package
shall be tracked.

Record:

- Recommendation
- Implemented Actions
- Outcome
- Impact
- Success Score
- Lessons Learned

Maintain a persistent guidance effectiveness ledger.

## Success Criteria

The system is successful when:

- External model usage decreases.
- Information quality increases.
- Decision quality improves.
- Daily objectives are consistently achieved.
- Weekly objectives remain on track.
- Monthly objectives remain on track.
- Autonomy increases within governance constraints.
- Strategic opportunities are identified earlier.
- Operational risks are identified earlier.
- The ecosystem continuously improves through measured learning and feedback.
