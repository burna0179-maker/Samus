# ADR-0005: Human-on-the-Loop Governance

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Externally visible, financial, legal, and irreversible actions have higher failure costs than internal analysis.

## Decision
Classify action risk and retain operator approval, quorum, suppression, or explicit enablement for higher-risk effects.

## Consequences
Positive: preserves operator authority and auditability.
Negative: higher-risk workflows are slower and depend on approval availability.
